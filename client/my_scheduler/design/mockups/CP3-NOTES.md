## Fix round 1
- The garbled "Planning, 2:00 PM-3:30 PM" over the title in the first t13-focus screenshot was a stale capture from the dropped `::before { content: attr(title) }` experiment; no `content: attr(...)` or visible-overflow pseudo-element remains in any stylesheet. Re-verified in the browser: computed `::before`/`::after` on a focused event are empty. New captures: t13b-focus-{planning,lunch,standup}-1280.png (normal, purple, dashed Allowed). The ring is a 2px page-colored gap plus a 3px mint ring; mint vs page is 12:1, page vs purple/royal fill is tested >= 3:1, and mint vs the royal fill is 3.49:1. Mint vs the purple token fill alone is 2.55:1, which is why the gap exists: the ring never touches the fill.

Conflict outlines are `outline`, so they are untouched; hover/focus rules replace the shadow with their own rings.
